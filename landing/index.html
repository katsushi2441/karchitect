Kurage Architect

From conversation to implementation-ready design docs.

"I know what I want to build, but the requirements won't come together." Kurage Architect grows that vague idea into a system design document you can implement from directly — by talking it through with AI. Open source, powered by a local LLM (Gemma 4).

FEATURES

What it does

💬AI elicits your requirements

Asks 1–3 focused questions at a time, separating decisions, proposals, assumptions and open questions.

🗺️Diagrams generated for you

Architecture, class and sequence diagrams in Mermaid, embedded straight into the design doc.

📄Hand-off-ready output

Export as Markdown, requirements JSON, HTML or PDF — a perfect input for AI coding sessions.

🔒Local LLM, private by design

The brain is a local Gemma 4. Your ideas are never sent to external AI providers.

🧭Spec-driven flow

Your design matures through 7 stages: discover → clarify → specify → plan → design → review → ready.

🛟Your answers are never lost

Even if the LLM fails, your answers are saved. Resume the design when it recovers.
